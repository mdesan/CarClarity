What do these specs mean?

Drivetrain. Front- and rear-wheel drive power one pair of wheels. All-wheel drive can power both pairs. Four-wheel drive is often used off-road.

Transmission. An automatic changes gears for you. With a manual, you change gears yourself.

Engine size. Engine size is measured in liters (ex. 2.4 L).

Fuel economy. MPG tells you how far a car can go on a gallon of gas (ex. 30 MPG means about 30 miles per gallon). MPGe is a similar rating for electric driving.
